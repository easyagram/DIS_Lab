Game can StartGame, StopGame, PauseGame, ResumeGame

Player can control SpaceShip, DefenseSystem, NavigationSystem

SpaceShip can StartMove, EndMove, Rotate, Fire, Destroyed, Respawn, ActivateShield, DeactivateShield
StartMove include MoveCommand, BurnFuelCommand
ActivateShield include ShieldUpCommand
DeactivateShield include ShieldDownCommand

Projectile can ProjectileStartMove, ProjectileEndMove, ProjectileDestroyed
Fire include LaunchProjectile
LaunchProjectile predates ProjectileStartMove

DefenseSystem can ActivateShield, DeactivateShield, AutoRepair
NavigationSystem can SetCourse, AdjustCourse, EngageWarpDrive, DisengageWarpDrive

Game must CheckCollision when StartMove or ProjectileStartMove
CheckCollision predates Destroyed, ProjectileDestroyed
Destroyed precedes Respawn

NavigationSystem must RecalculateRoute when AdjustCourse or EngageWarpDrive
RecalculateRoute predates SetCourse
